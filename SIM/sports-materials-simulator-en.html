What Are Clothing and Sports Equipment Made Of? — Fibers, Shoes, Gear, and Materials

Clothing and sports equipment use nearly every class of material except semiconductors and liquids — synthetic fibers such as polyester and nylon, natural materials such as cotton, wool, and down, rubber and foams, carbon-fiber composites, and metals such as titanium — each for a reason that suits its place, such as being light, drying quickly, bouncing, or absorbing impact.
