Fourier Series — Building Shapes by Adding Waves

Any periodic function can be built by simply adding up trigonometric functions with different wavenumbers. Why is that possible? Because the inner product of trigonometric functions with different wavenumbers is 0 — in other words, they are orthogonal to one another. You will watch the number of added waves grow one at a time, see the inner product really come out to 0, and see that a partial differential equation falls apart into separate problems, one for each wave. Last comes the story of how the Fourier transform turns differentiation into division (Thomas–Fermi screening).
