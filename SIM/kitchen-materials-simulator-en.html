What Is a Kitchen Made Of? — Tools, Appliances, and Materials

A kitchen holds nearly every class of material — the steel and zirconia of knives, the aluminum alloy and fluoropolymer coating of frying pans, porcelain, glass, and lacquerware tableware, PET bottles and aluminum cans, the crystallized glass and copper coil of induction-heating (IH) cookers, the insulation of refrigerators, and the stainless steel of sinks. Metals, ceramics, resins, rubber, composites, and liquids are all here, together with natural materials such as wood and lacquer. Each was chosen for a reason that suits its place in the kitchen, so that it can stand up to daily exposure to fire, water, oil, salt, acid, and cold.
