What Is a Smartphone Made Of? — Parts and Materials

A single smartphone packs almost every class of material into something the size of a palm — glass and sapphire, the organic molecules of an OLED, semiconductors such as silicon and gallium arsenide, functional ceramics such as barium titanate and lithium tantalate, neodymium magnets, resins, rubber, and even liquid electrolyte — each for a reason that suits its place in the phone. Select an area in the figure (or a button below) to zoom in and see each component’s material, composition, and “why this material?” This is a prototype.
