What Is a Car Made Of? — Parts and Materials

A single car uses nearly every class of material — steel, aluminum alloys, glass, ceramics, resins, rubber, and composites — each for a reason that suits its place in the car. Select an area in the figure (or a button below) to zoom in and see each component’s material, composition, and “why this material?” This is a prototype.
