Irreducible Representation = “A Set of States That Cannot Be Split Any Further”

Now viewing: the representationThe raising and lowering operators J± take you up and down the states like the rungs of a ladder. The coefficient is exactly 0 at the top and bottom of the ladder — and that is what fixes the dimension 2j+1.

Combining two multiplets: j1 ⊗ j2 = (j1+j2) ⊕ … ⊕ |j1−j2|. The product representation is not irreducible; it breaks up into a direct sum of irreducible representations.

Root Systems — Only Four Angles Are Possible Between Simple Roots

Now viewing: the Lie algebra (roots)A root = a weight of the adjoint representation = “the direction in which a raising or lowering operator moves a state.” Because the angle between roots is limited to 90°, 120°, 135°, and 150°, all possible simple Lie algebras are completely determined (Cartan’s classification). Click a root to run its reflection (a Weyl transformation).
