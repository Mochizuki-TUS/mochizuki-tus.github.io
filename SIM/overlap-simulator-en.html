Overlap Integral — How Much Do Two Atomic Orbitals Overlap?

Place one orbital on each of atom A and atom B, separated by a distance R, and compute the overlap integral S(R) = ∫φAφB dV. S is a quantity at the very foundation of the covalent bond, and it shows up in the secular equation, in STO-3G, and in band theory. In ① you see the picture of the overlap, and in ② the curve of S(R). You can choose not just 1s but orbitals all the way up to 4f.

All lengths are in units of the Bohr radius a0 = 52.918 pm (to avoid confusion with the symbol for atom B, and following Lecture 5, we write a0 rather than aB).
