Perturbation Theory — Switching On the Repulsion “Cautiously” to Solve He

Take a problem that can be solved (He without electron–electron repulsion) and cautiously mix in the term that cannot be solved, using the knob λ—that is perturbation theory. In ①, we switch on the electron–electron repulsion of He with λ and look at the first-order correction (Lecture 4); in ②, we use an exactly solvable two-level model to see when the perturbation series works and when it breaks down.

Energies are written in eV to match the lecture’s convention; ε1s = −13.606 eV.
