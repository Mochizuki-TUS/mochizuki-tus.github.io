What Is a House Made Of? — Parts and Materials

A single house uses nearly every major class of building material — concrete and steel reinforcing bars, wood, roof tiles of fired clay, metal sheets, glass, insulation, gypsum board, ceramics, resin pipes, and copper wire — each for a reason that suits its place in the house. A house is exposed to rain, wind, ultraviolet light, humidity, and earthquakes for decades, and people must still be able to live in it comfortably, so materials are chosen not only for strength but also for durability and for how well they block heat and water.
