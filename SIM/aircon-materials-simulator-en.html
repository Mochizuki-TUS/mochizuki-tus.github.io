What Is an Air Conditioner Made Of? — Parts and Materials

A single air conditioner packs in metals, ceramics, and semiconductors — copper tubes and aluminum fins that carry heat, a compressor in a steel shell driven by a motor with neodymium magnets, and power semiconductors such as silicon that control the electric power. It also contains a fan made of glass-fiber-reinforced resin, an ABS resin exterior, galvanized and painted steel outer panels, rubber vibration isolators, foamed-resin insulation, and even the refrigerant and refrigeration oil (liquids). Almost every class of material is used, each for a reason that suits its place.
