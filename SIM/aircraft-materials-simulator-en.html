What Is an Airliner Made Of? — Parts and Materials

A single airliner uses nearly every class of material — lightweight aluminum alloys with excellent fatigue resistance, even lighter carbon-fiber composites, high-strength steel, rubber tires and carbon brakes, windows of stretched acrylic and strengthened glass, and, in the engine, nickel-based superalloys and ceramics exposed to gas at around 1500 °C — each for a reason that suits its place on the aircraft. The range of temperatures is vast as well, from below −50 °C at cruising altitude to the hot interior of the engine.
