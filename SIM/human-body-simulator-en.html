Please note: This simulator is a simplified educational model that reproduces the average resting values of a healthy person. It does not diagnose or evaluate the condition of any real individual. The equations combine standard physiological relations (cardiac output = heart rate × stroke volume; mean arterial pressure = central venous pressure + systemic vascular resistance × cardiac output; pulse pressure = stroke volume ÷ arterial compliance) with tables of representative values by age. Actual blood pressure easily varies by 10–20 mmHg with posture, activity, emotion, meals, and measurement method. If you are concerned about your health, be sure to consult a medical professional.
