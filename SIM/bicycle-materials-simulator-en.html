What Is a Bicycle Made Of? — Parts and Materials

A single bicycle uses almost every class of material — frames of steel, aluminum alloy, titanium alloy, or carbon-fiber composite, stainless-steel spokes, rubber tires, resin saddles, LED semiconductors, glass beads in reflectors, and, on electric-assist models, neodymium magnets and lithium-ion batteries — each for a reason that suits its place, so that a vehicle powered by human muscle can be both light and tough.
