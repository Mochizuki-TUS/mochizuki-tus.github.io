What Is Stationery Made Of? — Tools and Materials

The small pieces of stationery on a desk use almost every class of material — natural materials such as wood and paper, ceramics made by firing graphite and clay, a cemented-carbide ball, metals such as stainless steel and brass, resins, rubber, and even liquid ink — each for a reason that suits its place. Every material is chosen with care so that the tool is cheap, light, and does not break with daily use.
