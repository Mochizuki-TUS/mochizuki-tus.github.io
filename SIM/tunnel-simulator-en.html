Quantum Tunneling — Transmission and Reflection Coefficients, and Wave-Packet Time Evolution

A particle with kinetic energy E is sent toward a box-shaped potential barrier of height V0 and thickness L. Classically, a particle with E < V0 is always reflected, but in quantum theory a finite probability of passing through remains.

Horizontal axis of the upper two panels = position x; the barrier occupies 0 ≤ x ≤ L.
