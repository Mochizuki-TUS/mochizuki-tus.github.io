University Mathematics— From Calculus to Partial Differential Equations: University Mathematics for Precocious High-School Students —

Based on the nine volumes of Yasuhide Mochizuki's handwritten notes, “Mathematics Notes” (Calculus, Linear Algebra, Fundamentals of Probability, Vector Analysis, Complex Analysis, Fourier Series and Transforms, Laplace Transforms, Ordinary Differential Equations, and Partial Differential Equations), this book is an educational resource that brings together, in a single volume, the whole of the mathematics studied at universities of science and engineering. The definitions, theorems, proofs, worked computations, and figures of the notes are included without omitting a single one; in addition, so that even precocious high-school students who want to understand university mathematics can read it, we have supplemented the text with explanations of how to read new symbols, the steps between the lines of formula manipulations, and concrete examples and figures.

Each section contains worked examples that omit no intermediate steps, and each chapter ends with exercises. The only prerequisite is high school mathematics, and where it is needed we have added links to the corresponding chapters of the companion volume “High School Mathematics” (AD 01). In the part on partial differential equations in the second half of the book, we go as far as applications to quantum mechanics (the Schrödinger equation and the hydrogen atom) and statistical mechanics (Planck's radiation law).

How to read this book
  • Part I (Calculus) and Part II (Linear Algebra) are the foundation for everything else. Read them first, in order.
  • From Part IV (Vector Analysis) onward, taking Parts I and II as prerequisites, you may start with whichever part interests you. Ordinary differential equations (Part VIII) are a prerequisite for partial differential equations (Part IX).
  • Unfamiliar symbols and Greek letters can be looked up in Appendix B (Symbols, Greek Letters, and Terms). Solutions to the exercises are in Appendix A.
