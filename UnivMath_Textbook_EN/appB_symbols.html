B.1 List of Greek Letters

Greek letters are used in great numbers in university mathematics as the names of variables, constants, and operators. The same letter often changes its meaning depending on the context, so simply learning how each letter is read (its name) will considerably lighten the burden of following lectures and textbooks. Note that 13 of the uppercase letters happen to have the same shape as Latin (alphabet) letters; such letters are hardly ever used in mathematics as independent symbols (and this book contains no examples of their use either). For the letters marked “not used in this book,” too, we have listed at least the name, so that you will be able to read other textbooks and papers in the future.
