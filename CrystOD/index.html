CrystOD#

CrystOD draws crystal-orbital diagrams: it takes a crystal structure and shows how the atomic orbitals combine — with full space-group symmetry labels — into the crystal orbitals at each k point, exactly the way a molecular-orbital diagram explains a molecule. Around this core it provides a complete symmetry toolbox: Symmetry-Adapted Linear Combinations (SALCs), basis functions, phonon irreducible representations, symmetry-adapted vibrations/modulations, magnetic (spin) multipole bases, direct products, isotropy subgroups, and group representation theory.

Four outputs, each linked to its section. Top row: the crystal-orbital diagram of ScF3 (crystod --diagram -c 221_PPOSCAR_ScF3 --co-left Sc --co-right F3) and the phonon irreps of SrTiO3 drawn on the phonopy dispersion (crystod-phonon --irreps -c 221_PPOSCAR_SrTiO3 --dim "4 4 4"); bottom row: the Brillouin zone of ScF3 (crystod-bz -c 221_PPOSCAR_ScF3) and the MO diagram of CH4 (crystod-mol --diagram --xyz XYZ_CH4.xyz). Every one of these inputs is bundled with the package, so each runs right after pip install CrystOD — see the “Try it” column below.

New in v0.4.9#

One line per feature: the command, what it gives, and the section that shows its output.

  • crystod-phonon -c POSCAR --element EL --irrep IR [IR2 ...] (no force data): every isotropy subgroup of the irrep(s) placed on the atoms of the element(s) as a symmetry-adapted displacement pattern, with the space group of the displaced structure identified again, VESTA arrows and the displaced structure; --qpoint K for every irrep at K (04-01).

  • crystod-mag -c POSCAR --element EL --irrep IR [IR2 ...]: every magnetic isotropy subgroup of the irrep(s) placed on the sites of the element(s), with its magnetic space group identified again, the MAGMOM line, a VESTA file and the magnetic cell (05-01); crystod-mag --parent SG --irrep A B ... gives the magnetic isotropy subgroups of coupled irreps (05-04).

  • crystod-phonon --mode-resolved-fc: the force constant and the effective mass of every phonon mode at the special q points, and the force-constant matrix of every irrep subspace (04-12).

  • crystod-mag --qpoint at q != 0: both phases of a complex order parameter and every order-parameter direction with ISOTROPY’s label and its magnetic isotropy subgroup (05-06); --subgroup-poscar CHILD aligns the directions with the domains of a distorted structure (05-07).

  • crystod-mag --qpoint ... --phonon-angular-momentum: the texture of the phonon angular momentum near Γ that the magnetic point group allows (05-08); --element A B analyses several magnetic elements in one run (05-09).

  • Commands whose result is a terminal report also save it, word for word, in a file named after the command (samm_*, saad_*, CORREL_*, salc_*, …); --no-files or CRYSTOD_NO_REPORT=1 turn this off.

In v0.4.9 the section numbers became two-level numbers CC-NN, CC the command group and NN the index inside it (for example 01-09 is crystod --unfold, 02-17 crystod-group --correlate-double-group, 04-09 and 04-10 crystod-phonon --unfold and --visualize, 05-04 crystod-mag --parent), so the example directories carry new names (Quick start has the table, the changelog the old and new numbers). The full list of changes and fixes, and the features of v0.4.8 (crystod --check-symmetry, crystod-group --correlate --supergroup-poscar P --subgroup-poscar C and the magnetic-cell POSCAR of crystod-mag --qpoint), is in the changelog.

Commands#

The package is organized phonopy-style: one main command plus one sectioned command per research domain.

Command

Domain

crystod

crystal-orbital SALC analysis (main command), --diagram (with dipole selection rules), --band/--dos, --visualize, --star-of-k, --extract-irrep (band irreps of a VASP run from its WAVECAR), --unfold (electronic band unfolding of two VASP runs onto the parent zone)

crystod-group

point/space-group representation-theory calculator (products, correlation tables, property tensors, isotropy subgroups, Landau invariants)

crystod-bz

Brillouin-zone plots (unit cell and supercell folding), special-k-point tables

crystod-phonon

phonon analyses (irreps, IR / Raman activity, fatband, LT bands, eigenvectors, modulation, vibration, subgroups of imaginary modes, band unfolding onto the parent zone, interactive 3D viewer of the modes by irrep)

crystod-mag

symmetry-adapted spin bases (cluster multipoles / SAMM); magnetic space groups, allowed responses and altermagnet classification

crystod-md

MD-trajectory analyses (ADPs, lattice summary, projection onto symmetry-adapted modes)

crystod-mol

molecular point groups, molecular SALCs, and MO diagrams (XYZ files)

crystod-xrd

powder X-ray and neutron diffraction patterns (Bragg peak list and broadened pattern); superlattice reflections of a distorted phase indexed on the parent, with k points and irreps

crystod-slab

surface slabs: every termination of given or of all distinct Miller indices, polar and symmetric tests, POSCAR files

crystod-search

Materials Project search (formula, chemical system, elements, ID) and POSCAR download

crystod-example

the packaged worked examples: lists the example directories and copies them into a working directory

Every feature carries a shared section number used consistently across this documentation, testsuite.py, and the example/ directories of the repository: feature CC-NN (CC the command group, NN the index inside it) is tested by python testsuite.py CC-NN (in a clone of the repository) and demonstrated in example/CC-NN_*, which ships with the package: crystod-example CC-NN copies it into ./example (crystod-example) (all but 01-07_check_symmetry and 01-09_band_unfold, whose VASP runs are not distributed).

What you can ask CrystOD#

Every command prints its answer to the terminal; the ones that draw something also write an HTML page, a PDF, or a VESTA file. A POSCAR (or an XYZ, or nothing at all) is the only input, and crystod-search fetches the POSCAR of any compound of the Materials Project. Every calculation runs offline, and crystod-search is the only command that goes online. The 3D viewers of crystod --visualize, crystod-phonon --visualize, crystod-bz and crystod-mol --visualize load plotly.js from cdn.plot.ly when they are opened, so the browser needs a network connection to display them; the crystal-orbital and MO diagram pages are self-contained. The “Try it” column holds the bundled example closest to each question: --example NAME copies the input files into the working directory, prints the equivalent ordinary command line and runs it (--example alone lists the names); crystod-example CC-NN copies a whole example directory, whose README holds the commands to run (11-01).

Which magnetic orders are symmetry-allowed?

crystod-md --adp --dim 4 4 4 --xdatcar XDATCAR

crystod-mol --diagram --xyz FILE.xyz

crystod-xrd -c POSCAR

crystod-xrd --example ScF3

crystod-xrd --parent PARENT.cif -c CHILD.cif
