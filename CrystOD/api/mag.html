crystod.mag#

Public magnetism API of CrystOD: the crystod-mag domain.

This module covers the symmetry analysis of spin arrangements. The spins on the sites of one element are treated as axial-vector degrees of freedom, the 3N-dimensional spin space is decomposed into irreps of the space group at a q point, and the resulting symmetry-adapted spin bases (cluster multipoles / symmetry-adapted multipole moments, SAMM, after M.-T. Suzuki et al., Phys. Rev. B 95, 094406 (2017) and Phys. Rev. B 99, 174407 (2019)) are classified as ferromagnetic (cluster dipole) or antiferromagnetic (net moment zero). At q = 0 an AFM basis is named after the next rank at which its irrep occurs in the magnetic multipole representations: octupole, 32pole, … for an irrep even under the inversion, quadrupole, hexadecapole, … for an odd one (the magnetic multipole of rank p has parity (-1)**(p + 1); Cr2O3, Cr: GM1- quadrupole, GM2- hexadecapole, GM1+ octupole). It mirrors the crystod-mag command, whose spin listings, VESTA exports and noncollinear magnetization input (VASP MAGMOM, Quantum ESPRESSO starting_magnetization/angle1/angle2) are built from these functions; the implementation lives in crystod.spin_basis.

Spin representation (crystod-mag --qpoint, and the survey over all special k points when --qpoint is omitted)

  • get_spin_representation: the little-group irreps at q and the axial-vector representation on the selected sites, ready for spgrep.representation.project_to_irrep.

Multipole classification (crystod-mag at q = 0)

  • get_multipole_rank_lists: for every irrep, the ranks of the magnetic multipoles (dipole, quadrupole, octupole, …) in which it appears.

  • separate_ferro_combination: splits the net-moment combination (FM, cluster dipole) off the projected spaces of one irrep; the rest are AFM. analyze_spin_basis falls back on the real form of the irrep when the copies spgrep gives (in a complex basis for the E irreps of trigonal and hexagonal crystals) do not separate cleanly, and turns the partners of all copies onto directions that symmetry fixes.

  • MULTIPOLE_NAMES: rank-to-name table (1: "dipole", 3: "octupole", …).

Magnetic groups and collinear classes (crystod-mag --qpoint, every exported component; --classify-only for the table alone)

  • analyze_spin_basis: the whole q-point analysis as data (SpinBasisAnalysis: decomposition, bases, the real spin field of every component and its magnetic group, responses and class); magnetic_summary_rows gives the rows of the summary table and magnetic_summary_notes the notes under it. At q != 0 SpinBasisAnalysis.directions lists the order-parameter directions of every basis in the magnetic cell (OrderParameterDirection: ISOTROPY’s label, magnetic isotropy subgroup, size, index, free parameters, the exported component, the coefficients in the basis components); direction_table_rows and format_direction_table give the * Order-parameter directions * block. With analyze_spin_basis(..., subgroup_poscar=CHILD) (crystod-mag --subgroup-poscar CHILD), SpinBasisAnalysis.subgroup places the bases at q != 0 on a distorted structure whose cell is a magnetic cell of q (SubgroupAlignment: the transformation of crystod-group --correlate, the folding of every basis onto the GM irreps of CHILD) and aligns every one-parameter direction with its structural domain (SubgroupSpinField rows: the irreps of CHILD a field is made of, its magnetic space group on CHILD, the angle of an aligned domain from the exported component, the moments on the atoms of CHILD); subgroup_table_rows and format_subgroup_alignment give the * Spin bases on the subgroup structure * blocks.

  • magnetic_space_group: magnetic space group of a spin arrangement (spglib): BNS number and symbol, OG number, type I-IV, the magnetic point group derived from the BNS symbol and checked against the operations, and the unified (UNI) symbols of both (MagneticGroupInfo).

  • macroscopic_responses: net magnetization, electric polarization (with a note when the crystal without spins is not polar), linear magnetoelectric and piezomagnetic tensors allowed by the magnetic point group (MagneticResponses); format_magnetic_responses prints them.

  • classify_collinear: ferromagnet, ferrimagnet, conventional antiferromagnet or altermagnet (d/g/i-wave, spin-splitting form, spin-degenerate planes) from the sign pattern of a collinear arrangement (CollinearClass), after L. Smejkal, J. Sinova and T. Jungwirth, Phys. Rev. X 12, 031042 (2022).

  • magnetic_point_group_symbol: the MPG symbol of a BNS symbol.

  • unified_symbol: the UNI symbol of a BNS number (B. J. Campbell, H. T. Stokes, J. M. Perez-Mato and J. Rodriguez-Carvajal, Acta Cryst. A78, 99 (2022)); unified_point_group_symbol: the UNI symbol of an MPG.

Phonon angular momentum (crystod-mag --qpoint ... --phonon-angular-momentum)

  • phonon_angular_momentum: the texture of the phonon angular momentum l(q) near GM that a magnetic point group allows, from l(A q) = B l(q): for each Cartesian component the lowest degree of a q-polynomial (s, p, d, f, g, … wave), its polynomials and the nodal planes single operations enforce (PhononAngularMomentum, AngularMomentumComponent); plane_normal= restricts q to a plane (a layer: nodal lines); phonon_angular_momentum_degrees: every degree at which a component is allowed (the orders of the angular-momentum current response to a temperature gradient), after L. Zhang and Q. Niu, Phys. Rev. Lett. 112, 085503 (2014).

  • phonon_angular_momentum_rows and format_phonon_angular_momentum: the * Phonon angular momentum allowed by symmetry * block of --classify-only (the paramagnetic crystal first, paramagnetic_point_group, then every component).

When that lattice is not invariant under the point group of the crystal (a primitive cell off the standard setting taken with --conventional, a 2x1x1 cell of a cubic crystal), the group found directly can be a subgroup of the true one. The structure is then also identified in the cell of its translations without time reversal, T_U (built by unitary_cell() from the pure translations among the direct operations), whose lattice is invariant under every rotation of the magnetic group: the direct result is kept when it is complete (its number of operations is that of the T_U cell times the number of T_U cells in the cell given), else the result found in the T_U cell is returned, with its operations repeated over the translations of T_U in the cell given (Cartesian rotations, translations in the fractional coordinates of the cell given), so that the counts keep the convention of the cell given. MagneticGroupInfo.crystal_rotations then come from a primitive cell of the structure without moments. On a cell whose lattice is invariant under the point group of the crystal (every standard cell) the direct result is returned unchanged.

Parameters:

  • lattice – Lattice vectors as rows, (3, 3) in Angstrom.

  • mag_symprec (float | None) – Tolerance on the moments (spglib’s mag_symprec; None uses symprec).

A MagneticGroupInfo.

crystod.mag.phonon_angular_momentum(group_or_rotations, time_reversals=None, *, plane_normal=None, max_degree=9)[source]#

The phonon angular momentum texture near Gamma that a magnetic point group allows, component by component.

The angular momentum l(q) of a phonon branch is a real axial vector, odd under time reversal. An operation g = (R, t) (t: combined with time reversal, s = -1 if t else +1) acts on the wavevector by A = s R and on l by B = s det(R) R, and symmetry demands l(A q) = B l(q). For every degree n the homogeneous polynomial vector fields of degree n that obey this for all operations are found as a null space (in coordinates in which every substitution q -> A q is an orthogonal matrix). For each Cartesian component l_i the result holds the lowest degree with a nonzero i-th component (s, p, d, f, g, h, i, j for 0..7), the polynomials of that degree, and the nodal planes through Gamma that single operations enforce: those whose A is the reflection in the plane and whose B reverses e_i (a mirror without time reversal whose plane contains e_i; a 2' along the normal when e_i is along it). The number of planes can be smaller than the degree (4': l_z ~ q_z, no plane), so both are given. P T makes every component vanish; degree 0 is the net magnetization. When the planes pinned by single operations are fewer than the degree, the other nodes are not fixed in position by symmetry. Phonon angular momentum after L. Zhang and Q. Niu, Phys. Rev. Lett. 112, 085503 (2014).

Parameters:

  • group_or_rotations – A MagneticGroupInfo, or Cartesian rotation matrices (n, 3, 3) of the operations of the magnetic point group.

  • time_reversals – The time-reversal flags (needed with rotations).

  • plane_normal – For a two-dimensional system (a layer), the normal (Cartesian) of the plane q is restricted to: the polynomials are in the two in-plane coordinates (q_x, q_y for the normal along z; q_u, q_v for a normal along no Cartesian axis, see PhononAngularMomentum.in_plane) and the nodes are lines.

  • max_degree (int) – The highest degree searched (default 9).

Returns:

A PhononAngularMomentum.

Raises:

ValueError – Rotations given without flags, or an operation that does not map plane_normal to plus or minus itself.

Return type:

PhononAngularMomentum

Example

>>> import numpy as np
>>> from crystod import mag

>>> rotations = [np.eye(3), c4, c4 @ c4, c4.T]          # the group 4'
>>> result = mag.phonon_angular_momentum(rotations, [False, True, False, True])
>>> result.component("z").text()
'l_z ~ q_z (p-wave)'

crystod.mag.separate_ferro_combination(spaces, representation, n_sites, realify_aligned=True)[source]#

Split the ferromagnetic combination off the spaces of one irrep.

At q = 0 an irrep can occur several times in the spin representation (2 x GM4+ for the three Ni sites of AlNi3); crystod-mag calls this function once per irrep label with all of its projected spaces. The spaces are first aligned to a common partner basis (an intertwiner with representation is found for every space, and complex circular bases are recombined into real ones), then the net moments sum_i S_i of the aligned spaces are combined into one unit vector: its unitary completion gives the unique combination that carries a net moment (the cluster dipole, printed as FM) and the orthogonal combinations with sum_i S_i = 0 (AFM, net moment zero). crystod-mag names the AFM combinations by the remaining entries of the irrep’s rank list (get_multipole_rank_lists): octupole, … for an irrep even under the inversion, quadrupole, hexadecapole, … for an odd one (Cr2O3, Cr: GM1- quadrupole, GM2- hexadecapole). When no space carries a net moment, or the completion does not separate cleanly, the aligned spaces are returned unchanged as antiferromagnetic.

Parameters:

  • spaces (list[ndarray[tuple[Any, ...], dtype[float64]]]) – Real basis arrays of shape (dim, 3N) (one row per partner of the irrep; 3N = three Cartesian components of N spins) that all carry the same irrep, e.g. the output of spgrep.representation.project_to_irrep for one irrep.

  • representation (ndarray[tuple[Any, ...], dtype[complex128]]) – The spin-representation matrices of shape (order, 3N, 3N) returned by get_spin_representation; used to align the spaces to a common basis.

  • n_sites (int) – The number N of spin-carrying sites.

  • realify_aligned (bool) – Rotate every aligned row by its own phase to make it real (needed for complex spaces). Pass False for spaces that are already real and transform with one set of real matrices, where a per-row phase would flip the sign of single partners.

Returns:

(ferro, afm). ferro is the basis array of shape (dim, 3N) with a net moment (None when no combination has one) and afm is the list of the remaining net-zero basis arrays; together they span the same space as the input.

Return type:

tuple
