crystod-md#

MD-trajectory analyses. Three mode flags: --adp (atomic displacement parameters as CIF), --summary (time-averaged lattice statistics) and --project (projection onto the symmetry-adapted modes of the parent cell, or with --yaml onto its phonon eigenvectors).

I want to …

command

get the ADPs of an MD run as a CIF file

crystod-md --adp --dim 4 4 4 --start-step 1000

get the time-averaged cell of an NpT run

crystod-md --summary --start-step 1000

follow the R4+ / M3+ amplitudes of an MD run and see whether they are single- or double-peaked

crystod-md --project -c 221_PPOSCAR_ScF3 --dim 4 4 4 --start-step 1000 --qpoint R M

check that the projected modes carry all of the displacement (sum rule)

crystod-md --project -c 221_PPOSCAR_ScF3 --dim 4 4 4 --qpoint all --no-histogram

compare the fluctuation of every phonon mode with the harmonic model (effective frequencies)

crystod-md --project --yaml phonopy_params.yaml --dim 4 4 4 --start-step 1000 --qpoint R M GM --temperature 300

project every arm of a star (the three M arms of a cubic parent)

crystod-md --project -c 221_PPOSCAR_ScF3 --dim 4 4 4 --qpoint M --star

46. ADPs from an MD trajectory (--adp)#

Example directory: example/46_xdatcar2adp (testsuite section 46); after pip install: crystod-example 46

Note

The example/... paths on this page are those of a clone of the repository, used from its root. After pip install CrystOD, run crystod-example NN (the number of the example directory) in your working directory first: it copies that directory and the shared test_POSCARs/ into ./example/, and the commands then run from there unchanged (see crystod-example).

What are the time-averaged structure and the anisotropic displacement parameters (ADPs, U_ij: the thermal ellipsoids) of an MD run? crystod-md --adp computes them from a molecular-dynamics XDATCAR trajectory of a supercell, with the constraints of the site symmetry, and writes them as a CIF file.

See also

Theory: Atomic displacement parameters from an MD trajectory — how the trajectory is folded onto the unit-cell sites and averaged over the symmetry-equivalent atoms, the site-symmetry projector that keeps an isotropic tensor isotropic in every cell, and the comparison with the original script.

terminal: the time-averaged unit cell and its space group, the ADP constraints of every Wyckoff position, Ueq of every site; file: ADP.cif with the _atom_site_aniso_U_* loop

How to read the output.

  • * Supercell info *: the atoms and the composition of the MD cell, the analysed steps, the unit-cell sites (groups) the atoms are folded onto and the replicas expected per group.

  • * Time-averaged unit cell *: the space group of the time-averaged unit cell (spglib, --tolerance) and its asymmetric-unit sites with their multiplicities and averaged fractional coordinates.

  • * ADP constraints per Wyckoff position *: the order of the site-symmetry group and the relations it imposes on U. The constraint text lists only relations the projector imposes (Uij=0, Uij=Ukl, Uij=-Ukl); a site constrained only by relations such as U12 = U22/2 (a two-fold axis along a of a hexagonal cell) reads general linear constraints only, and one without constraints no constraint.

  • * Trajectory unwrapping *: atoms that cannot be mapped onto their representative within --tolerance are left out of the statistics and named, with their 1-based XDATCAR atom numbers, in a WARNING: N atom(s) could not be mapped ... line of this block.

  • Ueq: that of the tensor written to the CIF (one third of the trace of its Cartesian form), so the table and the CIF always agree.

  • The CIF opens with the comment line # generated using crystod-md --adp and contains the cell, the symmetry operations, the asymmetric-unit sites, and the _atom_site_aniso_U_* loop, ready for thermal-ellipsoid display in VESTA.

The full trajectory

The full 41.7 MB XDATCAR of the run (4001 frames; not in the pip package) with --start-step 1000 analyses all 3001 frames of the same part and gives the same to 0.2 % (Ueq 0.005485 and 0.023521 A^2).

Options

Options.

option

meaning

--dim

the MD supercell size relative to the unit cell, given as three diagonal values or a nine-value diagonal matrix, quoted or unquoted (--dim 4 4 4, --dim="4 4 4", --dim 4 0 0 0 4 0 0 0 4; non-diagonal matrices are rejected)

--start-step

discards the first N MD steps as equilibration (default 0)

--xdatcar

the trajectory (default XDATCAR)

--output

the CIF file (default ADP.cif)

--format

the trajectory format (currently vasp only; LAMMPS support is planned)

--grouping-tolerance

how far an atom may sit from its site and still be grouped with it (default 0.1)

--tolerance

the symmetry tolerance of the time-averaged unit cell (default 0.1, a Cartesian distance in Angstrom); the same distance selects the site-symmetry operations of each site and maps every site onto its asymmetric-unit representative

Both fixed-cell and variable-cell (NpT, repeated-header) XDATCAR files are supported.

From Python

In Python, md.get_site_symmetry_operations(coords, rotations, translations, symprec, lattice=L) and md.build_symmetry_projector(rotations, lattice=L) take the lattice (rows = lattice vectors, Angstrom) for the Angstrom tolerance and the matrices M; without lattice= they keep the fractional tolerance and use R itself, which is exact only for the cells with M = R (Site-symmetry projector of the ADP tensor).

Based on script/xdatcar_to_adp.py by Ko Sato, with a fast built-in parser in place of the pymatgen reader.

47. Mode projection of an MD trajectory (--project)#

Example directory: example/47_md_projection (testsuite section 47); after pip install: crystod-example 47

How far does an MD run move along each symmetry-adapted displacement pattern of the parent structure, and with what statistics? --project gives the order-parameter time series Q(t) of every irrep, mode space and component at the chosen q points. Its typical use is a crystal near a structural instability, such as the octahedral tilts R4+ and M3+ of a perovskite: the width of the distribution of Q tells how large the fluctuations are, and its shape whether they are displacive (one well, a Gaussian about Q = 0) or order-disorder (two populated states +Q0 and -Q0).

See also

Theory: Mode amplitudes of an MD trajectory — how the amplitude Q is normalized and how it compares with the amplitudes of --modulation and of AMPLIMODES, the synthetic check, what the statistics measure, the discussion of the ScF3 run, and the sum rule and its comparison with the ADPs.

How to read the output.

  • irrep, space, component: the ISO-IR label, the mode-space number and the component number, as crystod-phonon --vibration lists them. At a q that is not time-reversal invariant each complex row gives two real patterns, components 1c and 1s, and of q and -q only one is projected (--qpoint all keeps the first of each pair; a -q given explicitly is reported as skipped).

  • <Q>, sqrt<Q^2>, <|Q|>: the mean, the root mean square and the mean absolute value of Q(t), in Angstrom; Q is the displacement norm per parent primitive cell carried by the pattern. In a structure with the parent symmetry on average, <Q> of every non-identity irrep is zero to within the sampling; a nonzero <Q> is a static distortion (or a sampling artefact of a slow mode).

  • ratio = <|Q|>/sqrt<Q^2>: sqrt(2/pi) = 0.798 for a Gaussian about Q = 0 (a displacive fluctuation in one well), 1 for two equally populated states +Q0 and -Q0 (order-disorder), 0.900 for a sinusoid.

  • kurtosis: the excess kurtosis about the mean, 0 for a Gaussian, -2 for two sharp states, -1.5 for a sinusoid, positive for heavy tails.

  • n_eff: the number of frames divided by the integrated autocorrelation time of Q(t) (summed to the first zero of the autocorrelation function), the number of effectively independent samples. The ratio and the kurtosis scatter by roughly 1/sqrt(n_eff); with n_eff below about 50 a single component says little about the shape. For a weakly damped oscillation n_eff is an upper bound.

  • shape: the histogram density at Q = 0 against its maximum (odd number of bins, symmetric about 0): below 0.5 with at least 10 % of the samples on each side, double-peaked (hint); below 0.5 with the weight on one side, off-center; else single-peaked; zero for a component that does not move and n/a for fewer than 20 frames. It is a hint, not a test: a coherent oscillation of large amplitude (a sinusoid) also reads as double-peaked.

For ScF3 at 300 K every irrep is single-peaked about Q = 0, as expected for the cubic phase, so the fluctuations are displacive. The tilts carry by far the largest amplitudes but are also the slowest modes (n_eff 16-19 for each R4+ component); the numbers are discussed in Tilt fluctuations of ScF3 at 300 K.

More on reading the output

  • * Site matching *: every atom is matched one-to-one to an ideal site of the parent supercell, by element and nearest image of its time-averaged position; the uniform shift (the mean offset of the averaged structure) and the largest remaining offset are printed, and the mass-weighted centre-of-mass displacement is removed from every frame (its rms is printed).

  • * Pooled components and norm |Q| *: for a mode space with several components, the samples of its components pooled (columns sqrt<Q^2> to shape); <|Q|> is the mean norm of the component vector, and |Q| ratio = <|Q|>/sqrt<|Q|^2> is compared with its value for an isotropic Gaussian in that many components (Gaussian: 0.886 for two, 0.921 for three). When the rms values of the components differ by more than a factor 3 (or one is zero), the pooled ratio, kurtosis and n_eff are not printed and the shape reads unequal.

  • * Sum rule *: the sum of <Q^2> over the projected components against <|u|^2> / N, the mean-square displacement per parent cell of all atoms after drift removal, the fraction covered and the number of commensurate q points covered.

Options and files

Options and files.

  • --qpoint: labels (R M X GM, the names crystod-phonon --vibration --list-qpoints prints), coordinates (--qpoint 1/2 1/2 0, any number of points in one list) or all (every q commensurate with --dim). The default is every tabulated special point commensurate with --dim, one arm each (GM, R, M, X for Pm-3m with an even --dim); the other arms of M and X are reached with coordinates, all or --star. A q that is not commensurate with --dim is refused.

  • --star: replace every requested q (labels, coordinates, or the default special points) by all arms of its star (crystod --star-of-k), each with its own block and file (--qpoint M --star gives the three M arms of Pm-3m, MDPROJ_ScF3_M_0.5_0.5_0.csv and so on); of an arm and its -q partner only the one listed first is projected, as with all.

  • --yaml, --temperature: the phonon-eigenvector basis and the effective frequencies, see below.

  • --irrep R4+ M3+: keep only these irreps (the dimension suffix is optional); the sum rule then covers that subset.

  • --start-step / --end-step: the inclusive range of frames, counted from 0 as in --adp and --summary; the CSV files carry the configuration= numbers of the XDATCAR.

  • --mean-lattice: convert the fractional displacements of every frame with the time-averaged cell instead of the frame’s own cell (no difference for a fixed-cell run).

  • -o PREFIX (default MDPROJ_<formula>): PREFIX_<q>.csv per q (step, then one column per component named <label>_<space>_<component>, e.g. R4+(3)_3_1; when several q share a name, the coordinates are added, MDPROJ_ScF3_M_0.5_0_0.5.csv), PREFIX_hist.pdf (twelve panels per page; --no-histogram skips it), and with --npz PREFIX.npz (steps, qpoints, qpoint_names, Q_<q> and labels_<q> per q).

  • --tolerance: the symmetry tolerance of the parent cell (default 1e-5).

Limits

Limits.

  • --dim must be diagonal and the MD cell must be --dim times the parent cell in the same orientation (each lattice vector within 10 % of the parent’s; the patterns are Cartesian in the axes of the parent file).

  • The parent must be the primitive cell, with the origin and setting whose ISO-IR labels you want (the frame rules of the labels apply: the octahedral tilts of SrTiO3 are R4+ with Ti at the origin and R5- with Sr; see Irrep labels and the frame of a structure).

  • An atom farther than 0.25 of the shortest parent lattice vector from every ideal site of its element, two atoms on one site, or a composition other than --dim times the parent’s stop the run with an ERROR: line.

  • Only VASP XDATCAR files are read (--format vasp; fixed-cell and NpT).

  • Without --yaml the basis is the symmetry-adapted displacement patterns, not normal modes: a mode space of a repeated irrep (the three GM4- spaces of ScF3) is one fixed orthonormal choice, and no mass weighting enters the patterns.

  • The drift removal subtracts the mass-weighted centre-of-mass displacement of every frame (its rms is printed), so polar optical motion is kept.

  • The ISO-IR names, mode-space and component numbers are those of crystod-phonon --vibration when the parent file is in the standard setting; --project works in the frame of the -c file (as the trajectory does), while --vibration converts the cell to the standard setting, so for a parent in another setting (for example rutile with its axes permuted) the two can name and number q points and mode spaces differently.

Python API.

project_trajectory returns a TrajectoryProjection (per q a QProjection with the column names, Q(t) as an array of shape (n_frames, n_columns) and the statistics; the site matching; the sum-rule numbers) and raises ValueError for bad input; qpoints="all" projects every commensurate q (md.commensurate_qpoints).

Phonon-eigenvector basis (--yaml)#

With force constants the trajectory can be projected onto the phonon modes themselves instead of onto symmetry-adapted patterns: --yaml phonopy_params.yaml makes the basis at every q the eigenvectors of the dynamical matrix, symmetry-adapted and numbered as crystod-phonon --modulation and --vector number them (1-based, sorted by the harmonic frequency, the partners of a degenerate level as --modulation freezes them; the force constants are used without the non-analytical term correction). The result is one time series per phonon mode, the normal coordinate, and with --temperature an effective frequency for every mode.

How to read the output.

  • mode: the mode number at q, 1-based and sorted by the harmonic frequency, as crystod-phonon --modulation and --vector number them; nu_harm: the harmonic frequency in THz (an imaginary frequency is printed as a negative number, as phonopy does).

  • <a>, sqrt<a^2> (A sqrt(amu)): the mean and the rms of the normal coordinate a(t) of the mode over the supercell, from the mass-weighted displacements; sqrt<A^2> per cell: the same per parent cell, A = a / sqrt(N).

  • ratio, kurtosis, n_eff, shape: as in the symmetry-adapted basis above.

  • nu_eff (with --temperature): the frequency of the harmonic oscillator that would have the same mean-square normal coordinate at T. nu_eff below nu_harm means the run fluctuates more along the mode than the harmonic model predicts; a soft or imaginary harmonic mode with a finite nu_eff is the anharmonic stabilization of that mode at T.

  • * Pooled degenerate levels and norm |a| *: one row per degenerate level (1-3 in the modes column), with nu_eff for the mean <a^2> of the level.

  • * Sum rule *: the sum of <a^2> over the projected modes against < sum m |u|^2 > of the supercell, in amu A^2; with --qpoint all the two agree to rounding (302.308248 amu A^2 for this run, relative difference 1.9e-16).

  • Files: the columns of the CSV files are named <mode>_<irrep> (1_R4+, 4c_DT5), the npz arrays a_<q> with frequencies_<q>, and the histogram file has one panel per degenerate level. The default prefix is MDPROJ_<formula>_phonon.

For the ScF3 run at 300 K the stiff F-dominated modes above 10 THz follow the harmonic model, while the pooled R4+ tilt has nu_eff = 0.744 THz, about half of nu_harm; this run and the force constants do not share a reference, so the comparison of the soft tilts says nothing about anharmonic stabilization (the table and the discussion are in Effective frequencies of the ScF3 run at 300 K).

Requirements and limits

Requirements and limits.

  • The parent cell is the primitive cell of the yaml (its atom order, positions and Cartesian frame; the trajectory must be --dim times that cell in the same orientation). -c is not needed with --yaml; if both are given, the -c cell must be that cell (lattice vectors and positions within the larger of --tolerance and 1e-4 A, atoms in any order), else the run stops with an ERROR: line.

  • A yaml without force constants (phonopy_disp.yaml) is refused the same way.

  • --temperature needs --yaml.

  • nu_eff assumes a classical run (no zero-point motion) in equilibrium, sampled long enough (n_eff: <a^2> has a relative scatter of about sqrt(2/n_eff)); it is only that mode’s frequency when the harmonic eigenvector is also a normal mode of the run (a coordinate that mixes in the run with another mode of the same irrep at the same q shows a frequency between the two).

  • Only commensurate q are reached, so the zone boundary appears only through the commensurate set of --dim, and the acoustic modes at GM carry nothing after the centre-of-mass removal.
