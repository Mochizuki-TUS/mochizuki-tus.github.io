Source code for crystod.phonon_mode_fc

"""
Mode-resolved force constants: ``crystod-phonon --mode-resolved-fc``.

From POSCAR + FORCE_SETS (or FORCE_CONSTANTS) of a supercell calculation,
at the special q points of the space group (the list ``crystod-phonon
--irreps`` surveys, or one ``--qpoint``), this gives the harmonic stiffness
of every phonon level and of every irrep subspace, written to
``mode-resolved-fc.yaml``.

Method.  With n atoms in phonopy's primitive cell (``primitive_matrix=
"auto"``), M = diag(m_i) the masses (amu) and D(q) the phonopy dynamical
matrix (eV/A^2/amu), the Fourier-transformed force-constant matrix is
Phi(q) = M^1/2 D(q) M^1/2 (3n x 3n, Hermitian, eV/A^2), taken from the same
phonopy object so that the phase convention is the one of the eigenvectors.
D(q) has the eigenvalues w (frequency sign(w) sqrt|w| in THz units of
phonopy) and the orthonormal eigenvectors e; the displacement pattern of a
mode is u = M^-1/2 e, normalized to 1 A (the Euclidean norm of u^ over the
3n components of the primitive cell is 1).  A q point with integer
coordinates (a reciprocal lattice vector, e.g. ``--qpoint 1 0 0``) is
reduced to Gamma first: phonopy's eigenvectors there carry the phases
exp(2 pi i G.r_j), which the uniform translations below do not.

(A) Per degenerate set, ``force_constant`` k = u^+ Phi(q) u^ = mu w, with the
    ``effective_mass`` mu = 1 / sum_i ``|e_i|^2 / m_i`` (amu); the sign of k is
    the sign of w (an imaginary mode has k < 0).  Degenerate partners of one
    irrep have the same k (Schur's lemma), so one value per set is given.
    ``force_constant_per_fu`` is k / Z, Z the formula units of the primitive
    cell (the gcd of the species counts).  At Gamma only,
    ``force_constant_translations_removed`` is k of u^ after its uniform
    translation (the mean displacement over the atoms, per Cartesian
    direction) is projected out and the rest renormalized to 1 A: a polar
    mode fixes the centre of mass, not the mean position, so its u^ carries
    a translation part.  It equals k for a mode without one and is not given
    for the acoustic set.  A Gamma set is acoustic when its translational
    weight, the mean over its orthonormal eigenvectors e of the squared norm
    of the projection of e on the three rigid translations (mass-weighted,
    sqrt(m_i) per atom), exceeds 1/2: about 1 for the acoustic set and about
    0 for an optical one, also with force constants that keep the acoustic
    sum rule only approximately (a frequency criterion would fail there).
(B) Per irrep present at q, the ``irrep_hessian`` block: the patterns u of all
    modes carrying that label (every copy, every partner) are
    orthonormalized (QR) into S (3n x d) and S+ Phi(q) S is diagonalized;
    the eigenvalues (eV/A^2), their trace and d are given.  The isotypic
    subspace is invariant under Phi(q), so the block does not depend on the
    basis; it is the Hessian of the energy restricted to the
    symmetry-adapted subspace.  At Gamma a block that contains uniform
    translations is also given with the three translations projected out
    first (``translations_removed``; d drops by their number).
    A degenerate set that carries two labels (an accidental degeneracy, such
    as the acoustic GM1 + GM5 set of P4mm) is split by the character
    projector P_X = (d_X / N_q) sum_g chi_X(g)* Gamma(g) (N_q the order of
    the group of q) built from the
    mode representation matrices of phonopy (``Irreps.ground_matrices``)
    and the character of a set at the same q that carries the single label
    X.  When every label of the set but one has such a reference, the
    remaining label gets the rest of the set (the complement of the known
    projections).  A set that still cannot be split (two or more labels
    without a reference, or one irrep twice) gets no k and mu, as they
    depend on the basis there: its labels are kept, its block is given under
    the combined name ``A + B`` and a NOTE says why.

Conventions.  k is the curvature per primitive cell with the pattern
normalized to 1 A over the primitive cell; it does not change when the
cell is enlarged, whereas k per formula unit does (it refers to the Z of the
primitive cell).  Units: eV/A^2 (1 eV/A^2 = 1000 meV/A^2), amu, THz.
With the amplitude A of an AMPLIMODES-type decomposition (``crystod-group
--supergroup-cif``) measured over the same cell, the harmonic energy of the
pattern is E_cell = k A^2 / 2.

Checks on every call: the eigenvalues w of D(q) equal those behind the
frequencies of :func:`crystod.phonon_irreps.get_irrep_labels` to 1e-6 of
max |w| (compared as w, not as frequencies, which amplify rounding near
zero) and the irrep blocks together span 3n (both raise ``RuntimeError``); the spread of k
within a degenerate set is below 1e-9 eV/A^2 and the number of negative
block eigenvalues equals the number of imaginary modes of that irrep
(Sylvester's law of inertia) (both add a NOTE otherwise).  NOTEs also mark
a q that is not a q point of the supercell (its dynamical matrix is
Fourier-interpolated) and, with NAC, a q so close to Gamma that phonopy adds
no non-analytical term.
"""

from __future__ import annotations

_ZERO_K = 1e-7  # eV/A^2: |k| or |kappa| below this counts as zero ...
_ZERO_K_REL = 1e-6  # ... or below this fraction of the largest |eigenvalue| of Phi(q)
_ACOUSTIC_WEIGHT = 0.5  # translational weight above which a Gamma set is acoustic
_EIG_TOL = 1e-6  # eig(D) against get_irrep_labels, relative to max |w|
_COMMENSURATE_TOL = 1e-6  # q . (supercell vector) this close to an integer: a supercell q
_SCHUR_TOL = 1e-9  # eV/A^2, spread of k within one degenerate set

class ModeForceConstantSet:
    """The force constant of one degenerate set of phonon modes at q.

    Attributes:
        band_indices: 0-based band indices of the set (ascending frequency).
        irrep_labels: ISO-IR labels with their dimension (``"GM4-(3)"``); a
            set of an accidental degeneracy lists all its labels, a set with
            no label carries a generic ``"irrep_N(d)"``.
        frequency: Mean frequency of the set in THz (negative: imaginary).
        effective_mass: mu = 1 / (sum over i of ``|e_i|^2 / m_i``) in amu,
            or ``None`` for a set split into ``components`` and for a set
            that cannot be split (its value would depend on the basis).
        force_constant: k = u^+ Phi u^ = mu w in eV/A^2 per primitive cell
            (the pattern u^ normalized to 1 A over the primitive cell), or
            ``None`` as ``effective_mass``.
        force_constant_per_fu: k / Z in eV/A^2 per formula unit, or ``None``.
        force_constant_translations_removed: At Gamma, k of the pattern with
            its uniform translation removed and renormalized (eV/A^2);
            ``None`` away from Gamma, for the acoustic set and for a split
            or unsplittable set.
        acoustic: The set is the acoustic set at Gamma: its translational
            weight, the mean squared projection of its orthonormal
            eigenvectors e on the three rigid translations (sqrt(m_i) on
            every atom), exceeds 1/2.
        n_vectors: Number of patterns behind the values (the set size, or the
            dimension of the irrep subspace of a component).
        components: For a set that carries several labels and was split by
            the character projector: one :class:`ModeForceConstantSet` per
            label (same ``band_indices``, one label each); else empty.
    """

    band_indices: list[int]
    irrep_labels: list[str]
    frequency: float
    effective_mass: float | None
    force_constant: float | None
    force_constant_per_fu: float | None
    force_constant_translations_removed: float | None = None
    acoustic: bool = False
    n_vectors: int = 0
    components: list[ModeForceConstantSet] = field(default_factory=list)

def _irrep_name(label: str) -> str:
    return re.sub(r"\(\d+\)$", "", label)


def _irrep_dim(label: str) -> int:
    match = re.search(r"\((\d+)\)$", label)
    return int(match.group(1)) if match else 1


def _orthonormal_columns(vectors, tol: float = 1e-6):
    """Orthonormal basis of the column span of ``vectors`` (SVD, rank cut)."""
    if vectors.shape[1] == 0:
        return vectors
    left, singular, _ = np.linalg.svd(vectors, full_matrices=False)
    return left[:, singular > tol * max(1.0, singular[0])]


def _supercell_text(phonon) -> str:
    """``"2 x 2 x 2"`` for a diagonal supercell matrix, else its nine entries."""
    matrix = np.rint(np.array(phonon.supercell_matrix)).astype(int)
    if np.count_nonzero(matrix - np.diag(np.diag(matrix))) == 0:
        return " x ".join(str(value) for value in np.diag(matrix))
    return "[" + " ".join(str(value) for value in matrix.ravel()) + "]"


def formula_units_of(phonon) -> int:
    """Formula units Z of phonopy's primitive cell (gcd of the species counts)."""
    numbers = list(phonon.primitive.numbers)
    counts = [numbers.count(z) for z in sorted(set(numbers))]
    return int(reduce(math.gcd, counts))


def _label_context(phonon, irt_table=None, prim_mat=None):
    """``(dataset, irt_table, prim_mat)`` of the labels of ``phonon``."""
    from phonopy.structure.cells import get_primitive_matrix_by_centring

    from .irreptables_compat import load_irreptables
    from .runtime_compat import get_symmetry_dataset

    dataset = get_symmetry_dataset(phonon.symmetry)
    if irt_table is None:
        irrep_table_class, _ = load_irreptables()
        irt_table = irrep_table_class(dataset["number"], spinor=False)
    if prim_mat is None:
        prim_mat = get_primitive_matrix_by_centring(dataset["international"][0])
    return dataset, irt_table, prim_mat

def mode_resolved_force_constants(
    phonon,
    q,
    *,
    irt_table=None,
    prim_mat=None,
    degeneracy_tolerance: float = 1e-3,
    q_label: str | None = None,
) -> ModeForceConstants:
    """Mode- and irrep-resolved force constants at one q point.

    Args:
        phonon: A ``phonopy.Phonopy`` object with force constants, built with
            ``primitive_matrix="auto"``.
        q: Fractional coordinates of q in the primitive reciprocal basis; a
            q with integer coordinates is computed at [0, 0, 0] (with a
            NOTE, and ``q_label`` ``"GM"`` when it is ``None``).
        irt_table: ISO-IR irrep table of the space group; built from the
            symmetry of ``phonon`` when ``None``.
        prim_mat: Conventional-to-primitive matrix of the centring; from the
            symmetry of ``phonon`` when ``None``.
        degeneracy_tolerance: Frequency tolerance (THz) of the degenerate
            sets (``--tolerance``, default 1e-3).
        q_label: Name of q for the result (``"GM"``, ``"R"``).

    Returns:
        :class:`ModeForceConstants` with one :class:`ModeForceConstantSet`
        per degenerate set and one :class:`IrrepHessianBlock` per irrep.

    Raises:
        RuntimeError: The eigenvalues of D(q) differ from those of the
            irrep labeling by more than 1e-6 of the largest, or the irrep
            blocks do not span the 3n mode space (an internal inconsistency).
        ValueError: The q point cannot be labeled (from
            :func:`crystod.phonon_irreps.get_irrep_labels`).

    Example::

        import phonopy
        from crystod import phonon

        ph = phonopy.load(unitcell_filename="221_PPOSCAR_SrTiO3",
                          force_sets_filename="FORCE_SETS",
                          supercell_matrix=[4, 4, 4], primitive_matrix="auto")
        result = phonon.mode_resolved_force_constants(ph, [0.5, 0.5, 0.5], q_label="R")
        first = result.band_sets[0]
        print(first.irrep_labels, first.frequency, first.force_constant)
        # ['R5-(3)'] -1.0867... -0.077309...   (eV/A^2: the unstable octahedral rotation)
    """
    from phonopy.physical_units import get_physical_units

    from .phonon_irreps import get_irrep_labels

    _, irt_table, prim_mat = _label_context(phonon, irt_table, prim_mat)
    q = [float(value) for value in q]
    to_thz = get_physical_units().DefaultToTHz
    notes: list[str] = []

    is_gamma = bool(np.allclose(q, np.rint(q), atol=1e-8))
    if is_gamma and any(abs(value) > 0.5 for value in q):
        # a reciprocal lattice vector G: phonopy's eigenvectors there carry the
        # phases exp(2 pi i G.r_j), the uniform translations below do not
        original = "[" + ", ".join(str(int(round(value))) for value in q) + "]"
        notes.append(f"q = {original} is equivalent to GM; computed at GM")
        q = [0.0, 0.0, 0.0]
        if q_label is None:
            q_label = "GM"

    labels, band_sets, freqs = get_irrep_labels(
        q=q, phonon=phonon, irt_table=irt_table, prim_mat=prim_mat,
        degeneracy_tolerance=degeneracy_tolerance,
    )
    freqs = np.asarray(freqs, dtype=float)
    irreps = phonon.irreps
    ground = np.array(irreps.ground_matrices)
    characters = np.array(irreps.characters)
    n_sym = len(ground)

    n = len(phonon.primitive)
    masses = np.array(phonon.primitive.masses, dtype=float)
    sqm3 = np.repeat(np.sqrt(masses), 3)
    minv3 = 1.0 / np.repeat(masses, 3)
    z_fu = formula_units_of(phonon)

    dynmat = phonon.dynamical_matrix
    dynmat.run(np.array(q, dtype=float))
    dmat = np.array(dynmat.dynamical_matrix)
    dmat = 0.5 * (dmat + dmat.conj().T)
    phi = dmat * np.outer(sqm3, sqm3)
    w, evecs = np.linalg.eigh(dmat)
    # compare the eigenvalues, not sign(w) sqrt|w|: the square root amplifies
    # the rounding of w near zero (acoustic modes, light atoms)
    w_labels = np.sign(freqs) * (freqs / to_thz) ** 2
    scale_w = max(float(np.max(np.abs(w))), 1e-12)
    deviation = float(np.max(np.abs(w - w_labels)))
    if deviation > _EIG_TOL * scale_w:
        raise RuntimeError(
            f"internal inconsistency at q = {q}: eig(D) and the irrep labeling differ "
            f"by {deviation / scale_w:.2e} of max |w|"
        )
    nac = getattr(phonon, "nac_params", None) is not None
    if is_gamma and nac:
        notes.append(
            "--nac: phonopy adds no non-analytical term at exactly Gamma (it needs a "
            "q direction), so the Gamma values are the analytic (TO) ones"
        )
    elif nac:
        q_norm = float(np.linalg.norm(np.linalg.inv(phonon.primitive.cell) @ np.array(q)))
        threshold = float(getattr(dynmat, "Q_DIRECTION_TOLERANCE", 1e-5))
        if q_norm < threshold:
            notes.append(
                f"--nac: |q| = {q_norm:.1e} 1/A (2 pi omitted) is below phonopy's "
                f"q-direction threshold {threshold:g} 1/A, so no non-analytical term is "
                "added at this q; the values are the analytic ones"
            )
    if not is_gamma:
        to_supercell = phonon.supercell.cell @ np.linalg.inv(phonon.primitive.cell)
        q_super = to_supercell @ np.array(q)
        if not np.allclose(q_super, np.rint(q_super), atol=_COMMENSURATE_TOL):
            where = f"{q_label} = {_q_short(q)}" if q_label else _q_short(q)
            notes.append(
                f"q = {where} is not commensurate with the {_supercell_text(phonon)} "
                "supercell of the force calculation (--dim): the dynamical matrix at this "
                "q is Fourier-interpolated from the supercell force constants"
            )

    # the three rigid translations: uniform in the patterns u, sqrt(m_i) in the
    # mass-weighted eigenvectors e (where they are exact eigenvectors of D(0)
    # under the acoustic sum rule)
    translations = np.zeros((3 * n, 3))
    translations_e = np.zeros((3 * n, 3))
    for axis in range(3):
        translations[axis::3, axis] = 1.0 / np.sqrt(n)
        translations_e[axis::3, axis] = np.sqrt(masses / masses.sum())
    eig_phi = np.linalg.eigvalsh(phi)
    zero_k = max(_ZERO_K, _ZERO_K_REL * float(np.max(np.abs(eig_phi))))

    def translational_weight(vectors) -> float:
        """Mean squared projection of orthonormal vectors e on the translations."""
        overlap = translations_e.T @ vectors
        return float(np.real(np.vdot(overlap, overlap))) / vectors.shape[1]

    def mode_values(vector, acoustic):
        """``(mu, k, k_tr)`` of one mass-weighted vector (k in eV/A^2)."""
        mu = 1.0 / float(np.real(np.vdot(vector, minv3 * vector)))
        pattern = vector / sqm3
        pattern = pattern / np.linalg.norm(pattern)
        k = float(np.real(np.vdot(pattern, phi @ pattern)))
        k_tr = None
        if is_gamma and not acoustic:
            rest = pattern - translations @ (translations.T @ pattern)
            norm = np.linalg.norm(rest)
            if norm > 1e-8:
                rest = rest / norm
                k_tr = float(np.real(np.vdot(rest, phi @ rest)))
        return mu, k, k_tr

    # characters of the sets with a single label: the projectors of a split
    reference: dict[str, tuple] = {}
    for index, label in enumerate(labels):
        if label is not None and len(label) == 1 and _irrep_name(label[0]) not in reference:
            reference[_irrep_name(label[0])] = (characters[index], _irrep_dim(label[0]))

    result_sets: list[ModeForceConstantSet] = []
    block_vectors: dict[str, list] = {}
    block_modes: dict[str, list] = {}
    unlabeled = 0

    def add_to_block(key, vectors, w_mean):
        block_vectors.setdefault(key, []).extend(vectors[:, c] for c in range(vectors.shape[1]))
        block_modes.setdefault(key, []).extend([w_mean] * vectors.shape[1])

    def component(label, vectors, bands, frequency, w_mean):
        acoustic = is_gamma and translational_weight(vectors) > _ACOUSTIC_WEIGHT
        values = [mode_values(vectors[:, c], acoustic) for c in range(vectors.shape[1])]
        ks = [value[1] for value in values]
        spread = max(ks) - min(ks)
        if spread > _SCHUR_TOL:
            notes.append(
                f"k spread {spread:.2e} eV/A^2 within the set of bands "
                f"{_band_text([b + 1 for b in bands])} ({label})"
            )
        k_tr_values = [value[2] for value in values]
        k_tr = None
        if is_gamma and not acoustic and not any(value is None for value in k_tr_values):
            k_tr = float(np.mean(k_tr_values))
        k = float(np.mean(ks))
        add_to_block(_irrep_name(label), vectors, w_mean)
        return ModeForceConstantSet(
            band_indices=[int(b) for b in bands],
            irrep_labels=[label],
            frequency=frequency,
            effective_mass=float(np.mean([value[0] for value in values])),
            force_constant=k,
            force_constant_per_fu=k / z_fu,
            force_constant_translations_removed=k_tr,
            acoustic=acoustic,
            n_vectors=int(vectors.shape[1]),
        )

    def projector_of(name):
        chi, dim_x = reference[name]
        return np.tensordot(chi.conj(), ground, axes=(0, 0)) * dim_x / n_sym

    for index, (label, bands) in enumerate(zip(labels, band_sets)):
        bands = [int(b) for b in bands]
        vectors = evecs[:, bands]
        w_mean = float(np.mean(w[bands]))
        frequency = float(np.mean(freqs[bands]))
        if label is None:
            unlabeled += 1
            generic = f"irrep_{unlabeled}({len(bands)})"
            notes.append(
                f"bands {_band_text([b + 1 for b in bands])} carry no ISO-IR label; "
                f"reported as {generic}"
            )
            result_sets.append(component(generic, vectors, bands, frequency, w_mean))
            continue
        if len(label) == 1:
            result_sets.append(component(label[0], vectors, bands, frequency, w_mean))
            continue
        # an accidental degeneracy: split by the character projectors; a label
        # without a reference set gets the complement when it is the only one
        names = [_irrep_name(item) for item in label]
        unknown = [name_x for name_x in names if name_x not in reference]
        parts = []
        if len(set(names)) == len(names) and len(unknown) <= 1:
            known_sum = np.zeros_like(vectors)
            for item, name_x in zip(label, names):
                if name_x in reference:
                    projected = projector_of(name_x) @ vectors
                    known_sum = known_sum + projected
                    parts.append((item, _orthonormal_columns(projected, tol=1e-3)))
                else:
                    parts.append((item, None))
            parts = [
                (item, _orthonormal_columns(vectors - known_sum, tol=1e-3) if cols is None
                 else cols)
                for item, cols in parts
            ]
            if sum(cols.shape[1] for _, cols in parts) != len(bands) or any(
                cols.shape[1] == 0 or cols.shape[1] % _irrep_dim(item)
                for item, cols in parts
            ):
                parts = []
        if not parts:
            combined = " + ".join(names)
            if len(set(names)) < len(names):
                why = "it holds one irrep more than once"
            else:
                why = (f"no set at this q carries {' or '.join(unknown)} alone, so the "
                       "character projector cannot separate them")
            notes.append(
                f"bands {_band_text([b + 1 for b in bands])} ({', '.join(label)}) are not "
                f"split: {why}; their k and mu depend on the basis and are not given, and the "
                f"{combined} block holds them"
            )
            add_to_block(combined, vectors, w_mean)
            result_sets.append(
                ModeForceConstantSet(
                    band_indices=bands,
                    irrep_labels=list(label),
                    frequency=frequency,
                    effective_mass=None,
                    force_constant=None,
                    force_constant_per_fu=None,
                    acoustic=is_gamma and translational_weight(vectors) > _ACOUSTIC_WEIGHT,
                    n_vectors=len(bands),
                )
            )
            continue
        comps = [component(item, part, bands, frequency, w_mean) for item, part in parts]
        result_sets.append(
            ModeForceConstantSet(
                band_indices=bands,
                irrep_labels=list(label),
                frequency=frequency,
                effective_mass=None,
                force_constant=None,
                force_constant_per_fu=None,
                acoustic=all(item.acoustic for item in comps),
                n_vectors=len(bands),
                components=comps,
            )
        )

    blocks: list[IrrepHessianBlock] = []
    for key, vectors in block_vectors.items():
        patterns = np.array(vectors).T / sqm3[:, None]
        basis, _ = np.linalg.qr(patterns)
        hessian = basis.conj().T @ phi @ basis
        kappa = np.sort(np.linalg.eigvalsh(0.5 * (hessian + hessian.conj().T)))
        block = IrrepHessianBlock(
            irrep=key, dim=int(basis.shape[1]), eigenvalues=[float(v) for v in kappa],
            trace=float(np.sum(kappa)),
        )
        # Sylvester: the signs of kappa are those of the mode k (= mu w)
        mode_k = np.array([
            w_value / float(np.real(np.vdot(v, minv3 * v)))
            for v, w_value in zip(vectors, block_modes[key])
        ])
        inertia_modes = (int(np.sum(mode_k < -zero_k)), int(np.sum(np.abs(mode_k) <= zero_k)))
        inertia_kappa = (int(np.sum(kappa < -zero_k)), int(np.sum(np.abs(kappa) <= zero_k)))
        if inertia_modes != inertia_kappa:
            notes.append(
                f"{key}: negative/zero eigenvalues {inertia_kappa[0]}/{inertia_kappa[1]} but "
                f"imaginary/zero modes {inertia_modes[0]}/{inertia_modes[1]} (Sylvester)"
            )
        if is_gamma:
            # the translations inside the block (singular values of basis+ T near 1;
            # exactly 1 under the acoustic sum rule, as T lies in the vector-irrep
            # subspaces), then the rest of the block orthogonal to all of them
            n_tr = int(np.sum(np.linalg.svd(basis.conj().T @ translations,
                                            compute_uv=False) > np.sqrt(0.5)))
            if n_tr:
                left, _, _ = np.linalg.svd(
                    basis - translations @ (translations.T @ basis), full_matrices=False
                )
                reduced = left[:, : basis.shape[1] - n_tr]
                hessian_r = reduced.conj().T @ phi @ reduced
                kappa_r = np.sort(
                    np.linalg.eigvalsh(0.5 * (hessian_r + hessian_r.conj().T))
                )
                block.translations_removed = IrrepHessianBlock(
                    irrep=key, dim=int(reduced.shape[1]),
                    eigenvalues=[float(v) for v in kappa_r], trace=float(np.sum(kappa_r)),
                )
                zeros = int(np.sum(np.abs(kappa_r) <= zero_k))
                if zeros:
                    notes.append(
                        f"{key}: {zeros} zero eigenvalue(s) left after the translations "
                        "were removed"
                    )
        blocks.append(block)
    total = sum(block.dim for block in blocks)
    if total != 3 * n:
        raise RuntimeError(
            f"internal inconsistency at q = {q}: the irrep blocks span {total} of {3 * n} "
            "dimensions"
        )
    return ModeForceConstants(
        q=q, q_label=q_label, band_sets=result_sets, irrep_blocks=blocks, n_atoms=n,
        formula_units=z_fu, notes=notes,
    )




def _special_points(phonon, irt_table, prim_mat) -> tuple[list[str], list[list[float]]]:
    """The special q points of ``crystod-phonon --irreps``, Gamma first."""
    from .phonon_irreps import _is_gamma_point, _special_points_in_label_frame

    names, points = _special_points_in_label_frame(phonon, irt_table, prim_mat)
    order = sorted(range(len(names)), key=lambda i: 0 if _is_gamma_point(points[i]) else 1)
    return [names[i] for i in order], [[float(v) for v in points[i]] for i in order]

def survey_mode_resolved_fc(
    phonon, *, degeneracy_tolerance: float = 1e-3
) -> list[ModeForceConstants]:
    """Mode- and irrep-resolved force constants at every special q point.

    The points are those ``crystod-phonon --irreps`` surveys (the ISO-IR
    special points, named in the frame of the labels), Gamma first.

    Args:
        phonon: A ``phonopy.Phonopy`` object with force constants, built with
            ``primitive_matrix="auto"``.
        degeneracy_tolerance: Frequency tolerance (THz) of the degenerate
            sets (default 1e-3).

    Returns:
        One :class:`ModeForceConstants` per special point.
    """
    _, irt_table, prim_mat = _label_context(phonon)
    names, points = _special_points(phonon, irt_table, prim_mat)
    return [
        mode_resolved_force_constants(
            phonon, q, irt_table=irt_table, prim_mat=prim_mat,
            degeneracy_tolerance=degeneracy_tolerance, q_label=name,
        )
        for name, q in zip(names, points)
    ]




def _num(value: float, decimals: int) -> str:
    # + 0.0 turns a rounded -0.0 into 0.0
    return f"{round(float(value), decimals) + 0.0:.{decimals}f}"


def _num_list(values, decimals: int = 6) -> str:
    return "[" + ", ".join(_num(value, decimals) for value in values) + "]"


def _band_text(bands: list[int]) -> str:
    """``"4-6"`` for consecutive 1-based bands, ``"5"`` for one."""
    if len(bands) == 1:
        return str(bands[0])
    if bands == list(range(bands[0], bands[-1] + 1)):
        return f"{bands[0]}-{bands[-1]}"
    return ",".join(str(b) for b in bands)


def _write_values(fp, item: ModeForceConstantSet, indent: str) -> None:
    # a set that cannot be split has no values (they would depend on the basis)
    if item.force_constant is not None:
        fp.write(f"{indent}effective_mass: {_num(item.effective_mass, 6)}\n")
        fp.write(f"{indent}force_constant: {_num(item.force_constant, 6)}\n")
        fp.write(f"{indent}force_constant_per_fu: {_num(item.force_constant_per_fu, 6)}\n")
    if item.force_constant_translations_removed is not None:
        fp.write(
            f"{indent}force_constant_translations_removed: "
            f"{_num(item.force_constant_translations_removed, 6)}\n"
        )
    if item.acoustic:
        fp.write(f"{indent}acoustic: true\n")

def write_mode_resolved_fc_yaml(
    results: list[ModeForceConstants],
    path: str = DEFAULT_OUTPUT,
    *,
    space_group: str,
    nac: bool,
    formula_units: int,
    special_points: list[tuple[str, list[float]]] | None = None,
    notes: list[str] | None = None,
) -> None:
    """Write ``mode-resolved-fc.yaml``.

    Args:
        results: The q points to write (:func:`survey_mode_resolved_fc` or
            :func:`mode_resolved_force_constants`).
        path: File path (default ``mode-resolved-fc.yaml``).
        space_group: Hermann-Mauguin symbol for the header.
        nac: Whether the NAC correction was applied.
        formula_units: Formula units Z of the primitive cell.
        special_points: ``[(label, q), ...]`` of the ``special_points`` list
            (default: the q points of ``results``).
        notes: Remarks on the whole run (the force-constant consistency
            NOTE), written as a top-level ``notes`` list; none by default.
    """
    from .phonon_irreps import _yaml_flow_texts, _yaml_qpoint, _yaml_text

    if special_points is None:
        special_points = [(r.q_label or "", r.q) for r in results]
    with open(path, "w", encoding="utf-8") as fp:
        fp.write(f"space_group: {_yaml_text(space_group)}\n")
        fp.write(f"nac: {'true' if nac else 'false'}\n")
        fp.write(f"formula_units_per_primitive_cell: {formula_units}\n")

            "  force_constant_per_fu: eV/A^2 per formula unit (force_constant / "

        fp.write("  frequency: THz\n")
        fp.write("special_points:\n" if special_points else "special_points: []\n")
        for name, q in special_points:
            fp.write(f"- q_label: {_yaml_text(name)}\n")
            fp.write(f"  q_position: {_yaml_qpoint(q)}\n")
        fp.write("\n")
        fp.write("mode_resolved_fc:\n" if results else "mode_resolved_fc: []\n")
        for result in results:
            fp.write(f"- q_label: {_yaml_text(result.q_label or '')}\n")
            fp.write(f"  q_position: {_yaml_qpoint(result.q)}\n")
            fp.write("  band_sets:\n")
            for item in result.band_sets:
                bands = ", ".join(str(b + 1) for b in item.band_indices)
                fp.write(f"  - band_indices: [{bands}]\n")
                fp.write(f"    irrep_label: {_yaml_flow_texts(item.irrep_labels, quote=True)}\n")
                fp.write("    frequency: %14.10f\n" % item.frequency)
                if not item.components:
                    _write_values(fp, item, "    ")
                    continue
                if item.acoustic:
                    fp.write("    acoustic: true\n")
                fp.write("    components:  # accidental degeneracy split by character projection\n")
                for comp in item.components:
                    fp.write(f"    - irrep: {_yaml_text(comp.irrep_labels[0])}\n")
                    fp.write(f"      n_vectors: {comp.n_vectors}\n")
                    _write_values(fp, comp, "      ")
            fp.write("  irrep_hessian:\n")
            for block in result.irrep_blocks:
                fp.write(f"  - irrep: {_yaml_text(block.irrep)}\n")
                fp.write(f"    dim: {block.dim}\n")
                fp.write(f"    eigenvalues: {_num_list(block.eigenvalues)}\n")
                fp.write(f"    trace: {_num(block.trace, 6)}\n")
                reduced = block.translations_removed
                if reduced is not None:
                    fp.write("    translations_removed:\n")
                    fp.write(f"      dim: {reduced.dim}\n")
                    fp.write(f"      eigenvalues: {_num_list(reduced.eigenvalues)}\n")
                    fp.write(f"      trace: {_num(reduced.trace, 6)}\n")
            if result.notes:
                fp.write("  notes:\n")
                for note in result.notes:
                    fp.write(f"  - {_yaml_text(note)}\n")
            fp.write("\n")

def read_mode_resolved_fc(path) -> dict:
    """Read a ``mode-resolved-fc.yaml`` file.

    The file is the one ``crystod-phonon --mode-resolved-fc`` writes; this
    is ``yaml.safe_load`` of it, checked for the ``mode_resolved_fc`` list.

    Args:
        path: Path of the file.

    Returns:
        dict: The file as a plain dictionary: ``space_group`` (str), ``nac``
        (bool), ``formula_units_per_primitive_cell`` (int), ``notes`` (list
        of str, only when the force constants fail the consistency check of
        :func:`crystod.phonon_irreps.check_force_constants_consistency`),
        ``units`` (dict of str), ``special_points`` (list of ``{"q_label", "q_position"}``)
        and ``mode_resolved_fc``, one entry per q point with ``q_label``,
        ``q_position``, ``band_sets`` (``band_indices`` 1-based,
        ``irrep_label``, ``frequency``, ``effective_mass``,
        ``force_constant``, ``force_constant_per_fu``, at Gamma
        ``force_constant_translations_removed`` or ``acoustic``; a split set
        has ``components`` with ``irrep`` and ``n_vectors`` instead of the
        values, a set that cannot be split has no values at all),
        ``irrep_hessian`` (``irrep``, ``dim``, ``eigenvalues``, ``trace``, at
        Gamma possibly ``translations_removed``) and, when there are remarks,
        ``notes`` (list of str, the NOTE lines of the terminal).

    Raises:
        FileNotFoundError: The file does not exist.
        ValueError: The file is not YAML or has no ``mode_resolved_fc`` list
            whose entries hold ``band_sets`` and ``irrep_hessian`` lists.
    """
    import yaml

    with open(path, encoding="utf-8") as handle:
        text = handle.read()
    try:
        data = yaml.safe_load(text)
    except yaml.YAMLError as exc:
        raise ValueError(f"{path} is not a YAML file ({str(exc).splitlines()[0]})") from None
    if not isinstance(data, dict) or not isinstance(data.get("mode_resolved_fc"), list):
        raise ValueError(f"{path} has no 'mode_resolved_fc' list; it is not a mode-resolved-fc.yaml")
    for point in data["mode_resolved_fc"]:
        if not (
            isinstance(point, dict)
            and isinstance(point.get("band_sets"), list)
            and isinstance(point.get("irrep_hessian"), list)
        ):
            raise ValueError(
                f"{path}: a 'mode_resolved_fc' entry lacks the 'band_sets' or "
                "'irrep_hessian' list"
            )
    return data




def _q_text(q) -> str:
    return "[" + ", ".join(_num(value, 4) for value in q) + "]"


def _q_short(q) -> str:
    """``[0.25, 0.25, 0.25]``, ``[1e-06, 0, 0]``: q with up to six significant digits."""
    return "[" + ", ".join(f"{float(value) + 0.0:.6g}" for value in q) + "]"


def _wrap_values(prefix: str, values, width: int = 100, decimals: int = 6) -> list[str]:
    """``prefix`` followed by the values, wrapped under the first value."""
    texts = [_num(value, decimals) for value in values]
    if not texts:
        return [prefix.rstrip()]
    lines, current = [], prefix + texts[0]
    for text in texts[1:]:
        if len(current) + 1 + len(text) > width:
            lines.append(current)
            current = " " * len(prefix) + text
        else:
            current += " " + text
    lines.append(current)
    return lines

def format_mode_resolved_fc(results: list[ModeForceConstants]) -> list[str]:
    """The terminal blocks of the q points: per q a ``* Mode-resolved force
    constants at Q *`` table and an ``* Irrep-resolved Hessian at Q *``
    table, NOTE lines after them.

    Args:
        results: The q points, as :func:`survey_mode_resolved_fc` returns.

    Returns:
        The lines (blank lines and the ``* Title *`` lines included).
    """
    lines: list[str] = []
    for result in results:
        name = result.q_label or _q_text(result.q)
        is_gamma = bool(np.allclose(result.q, np.rint(result.q), atol=1e-8))
        rows = []
        for item in result.band_sets:
            band = _band_text([b + 1 for b in item.band_indices])
            parts = item.components or [item]
            for index, part in enumerate(parts):
                label = part.irrep_labels[0] if item.components else ", ".join(item.irrep_labels)
                rows.append((band if index == 0 else "", label, item.frequency if index == 0
                             else None, part))
        width_band = max([4] + [len(row[0]) for row in rows])
        width_label = max([5] + [len(row[1]) for row in rows])
        lines.append("")
        lines.append(f"* Mode-resolved force constants at {name} *")
        lines.append(f"  q = {_q_text(result.q)}")
        header = (
            f"  {'band':>{width_band}s}  {'irrep':<{width_label}s}  {'freq (THz)':>10s}  "
            f"{'mu (amu)':>9s}  {'k (eV/A^2)':>12s}  {'k/fu':>11s}"
        )
        if is_gamma:
            header += f"  {'k(-transl.)':>11s}"
        lines.append(header)
        for band, label, frequency, part in rows:
            freq_text = f"{_num(frequency, 4):>10s}" if frequency is not None else " " * 10
            if part.force_constant is None:  # a set that cannot be split
                values = f"{'-':>9s}  {'-':>12s}  {'-':>11s}"
            else:
                values = (
                    f"{part.effective_mass:9.4f}  {_num(part.force_constant, 6):>12s}  "
                    f"{_num(part.force_constant_per_fu, 6):>11s}"
                )
            line = f"  {band:>{width_band}s}  {label:<{width_label}s}  {freq_text}  {values}"
            if is_gamma:
                if part.force_constant_translations_removed is not None:
                    line += f"  {_num(part.force_constant_translations_removed, 6):>11s}"
                else:
                    line += f"  {'acoustic' if part.acoustic else '-':>11s}"
            lines.append(line)
        lines.append("")
        lines.append(f"* Irrep-resolved Hessian at {name} *")
        width_irrep = max([10] + [len(block.irrep) for block in result.irrep_blocks])
        lines.append(
            f"  {'irrep':<{width_irrep}s}  {'dim':>3s}  {'trace':>12s}  eigenvalues (eV/A^2)"
        )
        for block in result.irrep_blocks:
            prefix = f"  {block.irrep:<{width_irrep}s}  {block.dim:3d}  {_num(block.trace, 6):>12s}  "
            lines.extend(_wrap_values(prefix, block.eigenvalues))
            reduced = block.translations_removed
            if reduced is not None:
                prefix = (
                    f"  {'  -transl.':<{width_irrep}s}  {reduced.dim:3d}  "
                    f"{_num(reduced.trace, 6):>12s}  "
                )
                lines.extend(_wrap_values(prefix, reduced.eigenvalues))
        for note in result.notes:
            lines.extend(
                textwrap.wrap(note, width=100, initial_indent="  NOTE: ",
                              subsequent_indent="        ", break_on_hyphens=False)
            )
    return lines

    return parser


def _resolve_requested(phonon, dataset, names, points, tokens) -> tuple[str, list[float]]:
    """``--qpoint`` tokens to ``(label, q)``, as ``--vector`` resolves them."""
    from .phonon_vector import resolve_qpoint
    from .runtime_compat import get_symmetry_dataset

    rotations = get_symmetry_dataset(phonon.primitive_symmetry)["rotations"]
    primitive, unitcell = phonon.primitive, phonon.unitcell
    isoir_context = (
        dataset["number"],
        (primitive.cell, primitive.scaled_positions, primitive.numbers),
        phonon.primitive_symmetry.tolerance,
        (unitcell.cell, unitcell.scaled_positions, unitcell.numbers),
    )
    try:
        name, q = resolve_qpoint(
            [str(token) for token in tokens], names, points,
            rotations=rotations, isoir_context=isoir_context,
        )
    except ValueError as exc:
        raise SystemExit(f"ERROR: {exc}") from None
    return name, [float(value) for value in q]


def _force_file_atoms(path: str) -> int | None:
    """Supercell atoms named in the first line of FORCE_SETS or FORCE_CONSTANTS.

    FORCE_SETS (type 1) starts with the number of atoms, FORCE_CONSTANTS with
    ``n_rows n_atoms`` (or ``n_atoms``); ``None`` when the first line is not of
    that form (a type-2 FORCE_SETS, an hdf5 file), and phonopy is left to judge.
    """
    try:
        with open(path, encoding="utf-8", errors="replace") as handle:
            tokens = handle.readline().split()
    except OSError:
        return None
    if not 1 <= len(tokens) <= 2 or not all(token.isdigit() for token in tokens):
        return None
    return int(tokens[-1])


def main(argv: list[str] | None = None) -> None:
    from phonopy import load

    from .phonon_irreps import (
        check_force_constants_consistency,
        check_nac_loaded,
        format_note_lines,
        nac_load_options,
    )
    from .phonon_vector import reduced_formula
    from .vasp_io import read_poscar_cell

    args = build_parser().parse_args(argv)
    output = args.output or DEFAULT_OUTPUT
    supercell_matrix = [float(value) for value in args.dim.split()]
    if args.readfc:
        force_sets, force_constants, force_data = None, "./FORCE_CONSTANTS", "FORCE_CONSTANTS"
    else:
        force_sets, force_constants, force_data = "./FORCE_SETS", None, "FORCE_SETS"
    # the cheap refusals first, before the force data are read
    directory = os.path.dirname(os.path.abspath(output))
    if not os.path.isdir(directory):
        raise SystemExit(f"ERROR: cannot write {output}: the directory {directory} does not exist.")
    if not os.access(directory, os.W_OK):
        raise SystemExit(f"ERROR: cannot write {output}: the directory {directory} is not writable.")
    if not os.path.isfile(args.poscar):
        raise SystemExit(f"ERROR: the cell file '{args.poscar}' was not found (-c).")
    unitcell = read_poscar_cell(args.poscar)
    expected = len(unitcell.numbers) * int(round(abs(np.prod(supercell_matrix))))
    found = _force_file_atoms(force_constants or force_sets)
    if found is not None and found != expected:
        raise SystemExit(
            f"ERROR: --dim {args.dim} makes a supercell of {expected} atoms from "
            f"{len(unitcell.numbers)} in '{args.poscar}', but {force_data} holds {found}; give "
            "the --dim of the force calculation."
        )
    with warnings.catch_warnings():
        # a conventional or supercell POSCAR: phonopy says that "auto" found a
        # smaller primitive cell, which is what this mode asks for
        warnings.filterwarnings("ignore", message="primitive_matrix defaulted to 'auto'")
        try:
            phonon = load(
                supercell_matrix=supercell_matrix,
                primitive_matrix="auto",
                unitcell=unitcell,
                force_sets_filename=force_sets,
                force_constants_filename=force_constants,
                **nac_load_options(args.nac),
            )
        except (IndexError, ValueError, RuntimeError) as exc:
            raise SystemExit(
                f"ERROR: phonopy could not read {force_data} with --dim {args.dim} and "
                f"'{args.poscar}' ({type(exc).__name__}: {exc})."
            ) from None
    nac_note = check_nac_loaded(phonon, args.nac, "BORN")
    fc_note = check_force_constants_consistency(
        phonon, force_sets_filename=force_sets, force_constants_filename=force_constants,
        unitcell=unitcell,
    )

    dataset, irt_table, prim_mat = _label_context(phonon)
    names, points = _special_points(phonon, irt_table, prim_mat)
    if args.qpoint:
        selected = [_resolve_requested(phonon, dataset, names, points, args.qpoint)]
    else:
        selected = list(zip(names, points))
    results = []
    for name, q in selected:
        try:
            results.append(
                mode_resolved_force_constants(
                    phonon, q, irt_table=irt_table, prim_mat=prim_mat,
                    degeneracy_tolerance=args.tol, q_label=name,
                )
            )
        except ValueError as exc:
            raise SystemExit(f"ERROR: q = {name} {q}: {exc}") from None
    z_fu = formula_units_of(phonon)
    write_mode_resolved_fc_yaml(
        results, output, space_group=dataset["international"], nac=args.nac,
        formula_units=z_fu, special_points=list(zip(names, points)),
        notes=[fc_note] if fc_note else None,
    )

    dims = " x ".join(str(int(value)) for value in supercell_matrix)
    print("\n* Structure *")
    print(f"  Space group: {dataset['international']} (#{dataset['number']})")
    print(f"  Formula: {reduced_formula(list(phonon.primitive.symbols))}")
    print(f"  Primitive cell: {len(phonon.primitive)} atoms, Z = {z_fu} formula unit(s)")
    print(f"  Supercell: {dims} ({force_data})")
    if nac_note:
        print(f"  {nac_note}")
    if fc_note:
        print("\n".join(format_note_lines(fc_note)))
    print("\n* Conventions *")
    print("  k = u^+ Phi(q) u^ = mu w in eV/A^2 per primitive cell, the pattern u^ normalized")
    print("  to |u^| = 1 A over the primitive cell; k/fu = k / Z; mu = 1 / sum_i |e_i|^2/m_i (amu)")
    print("  k(-transl.) (GM only): k of u^ with its uniform translation removed, renormalized")
    print("  Irrep blocks: eigenvalues of Phi(q) on the irrep subspace (eV/A^2); k < 0: unstable")
    print("  1 eV/A^2 = 1000 meV/A^2, the unit many stability analyses quote per formula unit")
    for line in format_mode_resolved_fc(results):
        print(line)
    print("\n* Output files *")
    print(f"  Mode-resolved force constants written to: {output}")


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
