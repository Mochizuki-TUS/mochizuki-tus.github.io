Educational Resources

Advancing the nation through the promotion of science

In both science and engineering, thinking deeply about a subject—especially in research—often requires a thorough understanding of the fundamentals. As stated in the founding spirit of Tokyo University of Science, spreading a deeper understanding of fundamental science can help both science and engineering flourish and, in turn, contribute to the nation's development. Subjects such as quantum mechanics may seem difficult at first, but simple model calculations offer a way to see, enjoy, and become familiar with them. I believe this experience is essential to deepening understanding. I hope these resources will support learners far beyond our research group and university.

These resources turn lecture topics that are difficult to grasp from equations alone into hands-on explorations in your browser. Change a parameter, and the results are calculated and redrawn immediately. No installation is needed, and they also work on smartphones.

Confucius said: “Learning without reflection leaves one confused; reflection without learning leaves one in peril.”

The simulators on this page and the advanced textbooks High School Mathematics (AD 01) and University Mathematics (AD 02) are in English. The linked explanation pages, the other advanced textbooks, and the scanned handwritten lecture notes are currently available in Japanese only.

Entropy of Mixing — Why Do Gases Mix Spontaneously When a Partition Is Removed?
